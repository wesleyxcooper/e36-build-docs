## Tools

- MIG welder (shop-mandatory)
- Angle grinder + flap discs (surface prep to bare metal)
- Wire brush, sandpaper, weld-through primer
- Floor jack and jack stands
- Transmission jack (subframe removal)
- Engine support bar or jack stand under oil pan (rear subframe work)
- Exhaust hanger tools
- Standard socket set

## Procedure

> **Sequence matters.** Complete rear subframe reinforcement first — it requires the largest disassembly and is also the window for trailing arm swap (03-suspension) and halfshaft kit install. Front subframe and strut towers follow after the rear is buttoned up.

### Rear Subframe Reinforcement 🏭 Shop-mandatory

1. 🏭 Remove rear interior (complete if 01-interior-prep is done), driveshaft, exhaust, **fuel tank**, and rear subframe before any welding begins.
   > ⚠️ **Pitfall:** Rear subframe reinforcement requires removing the rear subframe, exhaust, driveshaft, fuel tank, and rear interior before welding. Do not attempt to weld with the tank in the car. Reference: [Garagistic Complete Chassis Guide](https://www.garagistic.com/pages/e36-complete-chassis-reinforcement-kit-installation-guide) | [YouTube walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2mYdKzG10R4)

2. 🏭 Prep all weld contact surfaces: sand, grind, and clean every surface to **bare metal**. Remove all paint, seam sealer, and undercoating from every weld zone before any plates go down.
   > ⚠️ **Pitfall:** Surface prep before welding is the critical step — bare metal welds only. Sand, grind, and clean every contact surface. Weld porosity from paint contamination is invisible and structurally weak.

3. 🏭 Weld rear subframe reinforcement plates (BimmerWorld kit) — full E36 M3 spec coverage across the subframe mounting zones.

4. 🏭 Weld RTAB pocket reinforcement plates (BimmerWorld kit).

5. With the rear subframe still out, complete trailing arm swap, halfshaft kit install, and VAC diff cover swap per 03-suspension before reassembly. Do not close up the rear until all three of those tasks are confirmed complete.

6. Reassemble rear end: reinstall subframe, fuel tank, exhaust, driveshaft.

### Front Subframe Reinforcement 🏭 Shop-mandatory

7. 🏭 Weld front subframe reinforcement plates (RallyRoad kit).

### Strut Tower Reinforcement 🏭 Shop-mandatory

8. 🏭 Weld front strut tower reinforcement plates (BimmerWorld).
