## Procedure

### Roll Bar — Autopower U-Weld Kit

1. **Confirm the kit is the correct variant for E36 convertible body style (1993–1999).** The U-Weld kit includes: full main hoop, rear braces, diagonal brace, and harness bar. Verify all pieces are present against the Autopower kit contents list before the car goes to the shop.

2. **Strip rear interior completely.** Remove rear seats, carpet, door cards, and all rear quarter trim. The welder needs unobstructed access to the floor and package shelf mounting points. Weld spatter damages trim that stays in the car — protect everything forward of the B-pillar with welding blankets.

3. **Bring the car to a fabricator with roll cage experience.** Contact VAC Motorsports Philadelphia (2501 Snyder Ave, Philadelphia PA · 215-462-4666 · ask for John) — they are the confirmed local shop with E36 chassis experience and an in-house welder. Appointment-based.

4. **Bar positioning — welder to confirm rollover envelope before tack welding.** The bar must sit within the car's rollover protection envelope — the top of the main hoop must be at or above the height of the windshield header. This is the primary HPDE tech inspection criterion. Have the welder verify this measurement before any permanent tacks.

5. **Surface prep before welding — bare metal welds only.** Sand, grind, and clean every contact surface at the bar feet and floor mounting points. Paint contamination causes weld porosity — invisible and structurally weak.  
   > ⚠️ **Pitfall:** Surface prep before welding is the critical step — bare metal welds only. Sand, grind, and clean every contact surface. Weld porosity from paint contamination is invisible and structurally weak.

6. **Weld in the full kit**: main hoop, rear diagonal braces, and harness bar. The harness bar provides the rear attachment points for a 4- or 6-point harness.

7. **Post-weld inspection:** All welds should have good penetration with no porosity visible. Verify the bar is square in the chassis and that the harness bar is at the correct height for the planned harness mounting.

---

### HPDE Tech Inspection Requirements

Before attending any HPDE or drift event with an open-top car, the roll bar will be tech-inspected. Requirements vary slightly by organization, but the following apply universally:

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Weld-in only** | A bolted-in roll bar does not pass tech at most HPDE and NASA/SCCA-affiliated events. The Autopower U-Weld kit is designed for this — it must be welded, not bolted. |
| **Rollover envelope** | The top of the main hoop must be at or above the driver's helmet with the driver seated in the seat at ride height. Measure with your actual seat and harness installed. |
| **Padding** | Any bar surface within head-strike distance (typically within 36" of the driver's head path in a rollover) must be covered with SFI 45.1 rated padding. Confirm specific event org padding requirements before attending. |
| **Harness bar height** | Harness bar must be within a spec height range relative to the driver's shoulder height for correct 4-point/6-point harness geometry. Confirm with tech officials at your target org. |
| **Fabricator certification** | Some orgs (NASA, SCCA) require that the cage/bar was built by a licensed or inspected fabricator. Check your target org's rules before the weld-in appointment. |

> ⚠️ **Pitfall:** HPDE venues will tech-inspect your roll bar. The bar must be welded, not bolted, and must be within the rollover envelope. A fabricator with cage experience is not optional — a poorly placed hoop is worse than none in a rollover.

### Fire Extinguisher Mounting

8. **Mount Kidde Halotron 2.5lb fire extinguisher within driver reach** — driver's footwell, transmission tunnel, or behind the driver's seat are common mounting locations. Halotron is a clean-agent suppressor (no corrosive dry chemical residue) — required at virtually all HPDE and drift events.

9. **Use a proper mounting bracket rated for the extinguisher's weight.** The bracket must hold the extinguisher securely under track loading — zip ties and tape are not acceptable. Bolt the bracket to a structural mounting point (floor tunnel, seat rail anchor, or reinforced bracket to chassis).

10. **Verify extinguisher is charged** (pressure gauge in the green) before each event. Replace or recharge after any discharge.

## Seats & Restraints

### Seat — Recaro Pole Position ABE

**Why this seat:** Fixed one-piece GFRP shell (no recliner mechanism), dual-use design — runs with the OEM 3-point seatbelt on the street and a 5/6-point harness at the track. The ABE version has a dedicated shoulder belt passage that routes the OEM belt correctly regardless of seat height. Not FIA 8855-1999 homologated (FIA and ABE are separate Recaro products), but no local HPDE or drift org in this build's target region (MoE, Drift Evergreen, Club Loose, Pocono RSD) requires FIA seat certification — only valid harness tags are checked.

> ⚠️ **MoE rule:** "Race seats must have harnesses." If the PP ABE is installed at a MoE event, a harness is required — OEM belt alone will not pass MoE tech even with the ABE's street-belt capability. For MoE with OEM belt, OEM seats would be required instead. This is worth confirming directly with MoE before committing to the seat.

**Mounting — Brey Krause R-9240 (driver) + R-9241 (passenger):**

R-9240 and R-9241 are the E36-specific Brey Krause floor mounts for the Recaro Pole Position — driver and passenger sides respectively. Confirmed fit for both: *"Fits all BMW E36 3-Series models, including M3… Recaro Pole Position [and] Recaro Profi XL."* ([store.drivenasa.com R-9241](https://store.drivenasa.com/products/brey-krause-r-9241-passenger-seat-side-mount-adapter-bmw-e36-large-width)) Both bolt directly to OEM E36 floor points — no slider, no intermediate adapter. Include OEM 3-point belt buckle mounting point AND snap-in harness lap-belt hooks. DOT and SAE J385 certified. Because Sparco seats sit high with the R-924X series while Recaro seats mount low, tall drivers can generally run without spacers.

Seat height without sliders is the lowest possible configuration in the E36. After install, verify the rollover envelope with the seat in final position: driver's helmet top must be below the main hoop top with the driver seated. Measure before welding the bar is final — the bar height is set to the seat+driver envelope, not an arbitrary height.

Documented E36 usage: bimmerforums.com thread on race seats in E36 confirms Pole Position + floor-mount brackets in E36/2 chassis; German bmw-syndikat.de forum confirms PP ABE in E36 sedan with custom console. No convertible-specific documented install found as of Oct 2026 — floor mount points are the same for E36 convertible and coupe, so the R-9240 geometry applies.

**Install procedure:**

1. Remove both OEM seats — 4 bolts each (2 front / 2 rear, slide seat to access), disconnect yellow bulkhead airbag connectors, remove seats. Cap airbag connectors or terminate per airbag delete plan.
2. Bolt R-9240 to driver-side floor using OEM bolt locations; R-9241 to passenger-side floor. Use red threadlocker on all floor bolts. Note from BK install instructions: mounts are not symmetrical — keep driver and passenger mounts separate until installed in the car, as they are mirrored.
3. Attach Recaro side mounts to each PP ABE shell using supplied hardware. BK note: tighten the inboard (center console side) mount bolt fully, leave outboard loose until seated in the car.
4. Lower each PP ABE onto its BK mounts and bolt through the side mount slots. Set height and tilt outside the car first — rear hole sets height, front holes set tilt. Seat tilt adjustment: BK recommends keeping the front higher than the rear at the car's actual floor angle; measure outside the car before install. See [install PDF](https://bkauto.com/v/vspfiles/instructions/R9240.pdf) step 3 for the full adjustment sequence.
5. **OEM belt (street):** Route OEM 3-point shoulder belt through the PP ABE shoulder belt passage on both sides. Verify belt sits across the shoulder — not the neck — with driver and passenger seated at normal ride height. If geometry is wrong, adjust seat tilt before driving on the street. This is the critical street-safety check.
6. **Harness (track):** Route lap belts through BK snap-in lap-belt hooks, route sub strap through shell's crotch slot, route shoulder belts over shoulders to harness bar. Torque all harness attachment hardware to harness manufacturer spec.

### Harness

5- or 6-point, FIA tagged. **No 4-point harnesses** — rejected at MoE, Drift Evergreen, and all other local orgs. FIA-tagged harnesses expire at the end of the year stamped on the tag. All other harnesses (SFI) expire 2 years from born-on date. Do not run an untagged or expired harness — it will fail tech.

Shoulder belt angle off the harness bar is critical: bar must be within 0–5° of horizontal measured at the driver's shoulder height. Too steep an angle (bar too low relative to shoulder) pulls the driver down into the seat in a crash instead of back — the opposite of what's needed. Verify harness bar height against seated shoulder height after seat install.

### HANS Device

**Required at:** EMRA (Pocono) — mandatory per EMRA rulebook §3.4 whenever a racing harness replaces OEM belts. NJMP own events — required for in-car instruction sessions with harness. PCA at NJMP — mandatory with any harness.

**Not required at:** MoE HPDE, Drift Evergreen (Drums PA), Club Loose (Englishtown), Ready Set Drift (Pocono lot).

**Compatibility:** Bell K1 Sport SA2020 and SA2025 both have M6 threaded posts factory-installed. HANS-ready without modification.

The biomechanical case: a 5/6-point harness fixes the torso; the head can still swing forward under deceleration (clothesline effect). HANS tethers the helmet to the shoulder device so peak neck load is controlled. Running a harness without HANS is considered by most serious orgs to be worse than OEM belts with no harness. Plan for HANS from the start even if some events don't require it.
