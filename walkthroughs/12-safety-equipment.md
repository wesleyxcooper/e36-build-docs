## Procedure

### Roll Bar — Autopower U-Weld Kit

1. **Confirm the kit is the correct variant for E36 convertible body style (1993–1999).** The U-Weld kit includes: full main hoop, rear braces, diagonal brace, and harness bar. Verify all pieces are present against the Autopower kit contents list before the car goes to the shop.

2. **Strip rear interior completely.** Remove rear seats, carpet, door cards, and all rear quarter trim. The welder needs unobstructed access to the floor and package shelf mounting points. Weld spatter damages trim that stays in the car — protect everything forward of the B-pillar with welding blankets. Front carpet also comes out permanently — HPDE events require removal of anything not bolted in. Once carpet is out, install the **Elian Motorsport non-caged floor pan** (see Parts above) — the OEM carpet adds ~1.5" of floor height; without it the pedals sit at the wrong ergonomic angle and heel-toe becomes awkward. The Elian pan returns the floor to the correct height and adds grip tape for wet conditions.

3. **Bring the car to a fabricator with roll cage experience.** Contact VAC Motorsports Philadelphia (2501 Snyder Ave, Philadelphia PA · 215-462-4666 · ask for John) — they are the confirmed local shop with E36 chassis experience and an in-house welder. Appointment-based.

4. **Bar positioning — welder to confirm rollover envelope before tack welding.** The bar must sit within the car's rollover protection envelope — the top of the main hoop must be at or above the height of the windshield header. This is the primary HPDE tech inspection criterion. Have the welder verify this measurement before any permanent tacks.

5. **Surface prep before welding — bare metal welds only.** Sand, grind, and clean every contact surface at the bar feet and floor mounting points. Paint contamination causes weld porosity — invisible and structurally weak.  
   > ⚠️ **Pitfall:** Surface prep before welding is the critical step — bare metal welds only. Sand, grind, and clean every contact surface. Weld porosity from paint contamination is invisible and structurally weak.

6. **Weld in the full kit**: main hoop, rear diagonal braces, and harness bar. The harness bar provides the rear attachment points for a 5- or 6-point harness.

7. **Post-weld inspection:** All welds should have good penetration with no porosity visible. Verify the bar is square in the chassis and that the harness bar is at the correct height for the planned harness mounting.

---

### HPDE Tech Inspection Requirements

Before attending any HPDE or drift event with an open-top car, the roll bar will be tech-inspected. Requirements vary slightly by organization, but the following apply universally:

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Weld-in only** | A bolted-in roll bar does not pass tech at most HPDE and NASA/SCCA-affiliated events. The Autopower U-Weld kit is designed for this — it must be welded, not bolted. |
| **Rollover envelope** | The top of the main hoop must be at or above the driver's helmet with the driver seated in the seat at ride height. Measure with your actual seat and harness installed. |
| **Padding** | Any bar surface within head-strike distance (typically within 36" of the driver's head path in a rollover) must be covered with SFI 45.1 rated padding. Confirm specific event org padding requirements before attending. |
| **Harness bar height** | Harness bar must be within a spec height range relative to the driver's shoulder height for correct 4-point/6-point harness geometry. Confirm with tech officials at your target org. |
| **Fabricator certification** | Some orgs (NASA, SCCA) require that the cage/bar was built by a licensed or inspected fabricator. Check your target org's rules before the weld-in appointment. |

> ⚠️ **Pitfall:** HPDE venues will tech-inspect your roll bar. The bar must be welded, not bolted, and must be within the rollover envelope. A fabricator with cage experience is not optional — a poorly placed hoop is worse than none in a rollover.

### Fire Extinguisher Mounting

8. **Mount Kidde Halotron 2.5lb fire extinguisher within driver reach** — clamped to the roll bar main hoop once the bar is welded in (Phase 1) via a tube-clamp extinguisher mount (e.g. 1.75" billet clamp). Interim before the bar exists: footwell or transmission tunnel mount. Halotron is a clean-agent suppressor (no corrosive dry chemical residue) — required at virtually all HPDE and drift events.

9. **Use a proper mounting bracket rated for the extinguisher's weight.** The bracket must hold the extinguisher securely under track loading — zip ties and tape are not acceptable. Bolt the bracket to a structural mounting point (roll bar, floor tunnel, or reinforced bracket to chassis). Note: OEM-seat-bolt brackets (Kap Industries, The Bracketeer) do not apply — the build replaces the factory seats with side-mount buckets on aftermarket floor adapters, and an Element E50 (32 mm dia) does not fit a standard 80 mm bottle cradle anyway. If an Element E50 is added as a supplement, mount it to the roll bar with Element's E50 roll-bar mount (~$59, [elementfire.com](https://elementfire.com/collections/element-accessories)) or a Renegade billet side-mount seat bracket.

10. **Verify extinguisher is charged** (pressure gauge in the green) before each event. Replace or recharge after any discharge.

**Install procedure — Option A (VAC floor adapters + slider + Recaro side mounts):**

> ⚠️ Call VAC (215) 462-4666 before purchasing to confirm PP ABE mounting width (427–440mm) fits their side mount brackets and to get their slider recommendation for this seat.

1. Remove both OEM seats — 4 bolts each (2 front / 2 rear, slide seat to access), disconnect yellow bulkhead airbag connectors, remove seats. Cap airbag connectors or terminate per airbag delete plan.
2. Bolt VAC billet floor mount adapters to OEM E36 floor points — driver and passenger sides. Keep hardware loose for final positioning. Carpet/insulation can compress under the adapter without issue; use a washer under each mount point if preferred.
3. Attach Recaro steel side mounts to each PP ABE shell. Position the seat assembly on the VAC floor adapters (or on the slider if using a slider between adapter and side mount).
4. Adjust fore/aft position for both drivers before final torque. Seat height is set by the adapter stack height; lateral centering is set via the side-to-side slotted adjustability in the VAC side mount brackets.
5. Torque all floor bolts with red threadlocker. Verify the seat does not contact the B-pillar or center console at any fore/aft position in the slider travel.
6. **Roll bar height check:** With both seats installed at final height, have the 6' driver sit fully helmeted in their normal driving position. Measure helmet top height. Set roll bar ≥2" above this before welding. The bar height is determined by the seat-at-highest-slider-position envelope, not an arbitrary dimension — weld after seat install, not before.
7. **OEM belt (street):** Route OEM 3-point shoulder belt through the PP ABE shoulder belt passage. Verify belt sits across the shoulder — not the neck — with driver and passenger seated. Adjust seat tilt if geometry is wrong before driving on the street.
8. **Harness (track):** Route lap belts through side mount attachment points, sub strap through shell's crotch slot, shoulder belts over shoulders to harness bar. Torque all harness hardware to harness manufacturer spec.

### Harness

5- or 6-point, SFI- or FIA-tagged. **No 4-point harnesses** — rejected at MoE ([moehpde.com/faq](https://moehpde.com/faq)) and illegal at EMRA (Time Trial §3.3). A 4-point has no anti-submarine strap, and submarining is the failure mode Schroth warns about for 4-point belts ([Schroth instructions](https://www.schroth.com/en/racing/service/installation-instructions/?file=files%2Fdefault%2Fpdf%2Fracing%2Finstallation-instructions%2Fracing-belts%2Fea_8-2_online_2025-04.pdf)). SFI harnesses expire 2 years from the manufacture date; FIA harnesses are good until the end of the year stamped on the tag (NJMP and PCA: 5 years). Do not run an untagged or expired harness — it will fail tech.

Shoulder belts must run horizontally from the shoulders or slope down no more than 20° (EMRA §2.6; [Club Loose rulebook](https://clubloose.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Club-Loose-Rules-2026.pdf); Schroth). Mount them to the roll bar's harness bar, not the seat. A bar that is too low relative to the shoulder pulls the driver down into the seat in a crash instead of back. Verify harness bar height against seated shoulder height after seat install. Lap belts and the sub strap mount to the chassis, not the seat or seat rails (Club Loose rulebook; [PCA HPDE Minimum Standards](https://mediaassets.pca.org/docs/formsanddocs/94/2026%20HPDE%20Minimum%20Standards.pdf)).

**5-point vs 6-point:**

| | 5-point | 6-point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sub strap | One, centered between the legs | Two, against the inner thighs |
| Floor anchors | One center sub-strap anchor | Two sub-strap anchors 4–6 in apart, 2–3 in either side of centerline ([Grassroots Motorsports](https://grassrootsmotorsports.com/articles/strap-how-properly-install-racing-harness/)) |
| Comfort / ease | Simpler to buckle; common choice for HPDE ([ThreePiece](https://www.threepiece.us/blog/racing-harness-guide-4-vs-5-vs-6-point-explained/)) | Sub straps avoid the groin; more to buckle at entry and exit |
| Rules | Accepted at every org checked: EMRA requires five or more anchor points; PCA treats five, six, and seven point as equal; Club Loose allows 4–6 point and recommends 5-point for caged cars | Same |

Neither is safer if installed wrong: an improperly installed 6-point is not better than a properly installed 5-point ([Speedway Motors](https://www.speedwaymotors.com/the-toolbox/how-to-choose-a-racing-harness/29453)). Choose by the seat's sub-strap slot(s) and the anchor points available; the VAC lap-belt bracket kit ($39.95) and anti-sub mount ($69.95) support 4–6 point belts ([VAC floor adapter page](https://store.vacmotorsports.com/vac-motorsports-billet-floor-mount-adapter-bmw-e36e46-p559.aspx)).

### HANS Device

**Required at:** EMRA (Pocono) — Time Trial §3.4 whenever a racing harness replaces OEM belts; always in Road Racing ([EMRA rules](https://www.emraracing.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/EMRA-Rules-v3.29.22.1.pdf)). NJMP — tech form: "Must show HANS if using a harness system"; also required for in-car instruction in all groups except Novice ([NJMP tech form](https://njmp.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NJMP-Tech-Form-2026.pdf)). PCA at NJMP — required for all occupants of any car with an aftermarket harness and roll bar or cage ([Schattenbaum event requirements](https://www.motorsportreg.com/events/schattenbaum-2026-hpde-calendar-njmp-pca-855091)). Club Loose A group — a containment/head-restraint seat or a HANS (either one satisfies it).

**Not found in the rules:** MoE HPDE (FAQ does not mention HANS; MoE also runs at NJMP — confirm with MoE), Drift Evergreen (Drums PA), Club Loose B and C groups (Englishtown), Ready Set Drift (Pocono lot; rulebook excerpt does not mention HANS).

**Helmet compatibility:** The Bell K1 Sport (SA2020 and SA2025) has M6 threaded terminals built into the shell and is homologated for use with approved head and neck devices ([Bell](https://www.us.bellracing.com/p/k1-sport-sa20/)). The helmet anchors and tethers ship with the HANS device and screw into the terminals with threadlocker; no drilling ([HANS quick start guide](https://documents.hansdevice.com/techlibrary_hans_2022_qsg.pdf)).

**Device spec:** HANS III or IV, 20° (HANS recommends 20° for upright sedan and sports-car seats), Medium or Large per HANS's size chart, FIA/SFI labeled, post-anchor tethers (post anchor and Quick Release M6 are SFI and FIA approved; Quick Click is SFI only) ([Fast Racer](https://www.fastracer.com/a/answers/7526093/What-types-of-HANS-anchors-are-there)). A-1 Racing: HANS III 20° $449.95–$493.95, HANS IV 20° $459.95 ([A-1 HANS catalog](https://www.a1racing.com/search.aspx?manufacturer=107)). The seat needs shoulder slots that let the belts run freely, at least 25 mm between the back of the collar and the top of the seat, and a collar angle of 60–90° from horizontal with the harness tight ([FIA HANS guide](https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/hans_guide_and_installation_spec_19.03.2022.pdf)).

The biomechanical case: a 5/6-point harness fixes the torso; the head can still swing forward under deceleration (clothesline effect). HANS tethers the helmet to the shoulder device so peak neck load is controlled. Running a harness without HANS is considered by most serious orgs to be worse than OEM belts with no harness. Plan for HANS from the start even if some events don't require it.
