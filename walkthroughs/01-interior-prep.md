# Phase 1: Interior Strip & Prep

**Phase:** 1  
**System tags:** `[INTERIOR]` `[CHASSIS]`  
**Shop-mandatory:** No  
**Prerequisites:** Car acquired and insured (Phase 0 complete).

## Parts

None — this is a strip-down task. No new parts required.

## Tools

- Trim removal tools (plastic pry bars, clip pullers)
- Socket set and ratchet
- Camera / phone (documentation before any wiring is touched)
- Label maker or tape + marker (harness labeling)

## Procedure

1. ✅ Remove rear seats, carpet, trunk interior, and spare tire.

2. ✅ Remove soft top mechanism if it is in the way — it is **bolted, not welded**, and can be removed cleanly as a unit.

3. ✅ **Photograph all wiring connectors and harness routing before touching anything.**

   This step is mandatory, not optional. The interior harness photograph record becomes the reference for every subsequent electrical task. Capture:
   - All connector locations and their mating halves
   - Harness routing through the B-pillars and under the carpet
   - Any existing splices or non-factory wiring
   > ⚠️ **Pitfall:** If you skip the documentation step, you will spend hours later tracing wires you could have photographed in 20 minutes. Do this before the first connector is unplugged.
